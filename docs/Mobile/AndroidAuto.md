# Android Auto

The Android app works with Android Auto. Install it from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gooseberrydevelopment.pinepods), [IzzyOnDroid](https://apt.izzysoft.de/fdroid/index/apk/com.gooseberrydevelopment.pinepods) or [Obtainium](https://github.com/madeofpendletonwool/PinePods/releases), sign in to your server, and connect your phone to the car. Pinepods appears with the other media apps.

The media browser shows:

| Menu | |
|---|---|
| **Current** | Episodes you're in the middle of. |
| **Queue** | Your queue. |
| **Downloads** | Episodes downloaded to the phone. |
| **More** | **Saved**, **History**, **Podcasts** and **Playlists**. |

You can also search by voice or from the car's search button.

The car's controls, the steering-wheel buttons and the notification controls all control the same playback. Skipping forward and back uses the **Fast-forward interval** and **Rewind interval** from the app's **Settings**.

Download episodes before a drive (see [Downloads](../Features/Downloads.md#device-downloads-mobile)) so playback doesn't depend on mobile signal.
