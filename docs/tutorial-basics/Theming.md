# Themes

Pinepods comes with 25 themes, light and dark. Pick one under **Settings → Change Theme**: click a theme's card and it applies straight away. **Show N more** expands the full list.

Your theme is saved to your account, so the web app, the desktop app and the mobile app all use the one you chose last. Changing it on the mobile app (**Settings** in the app) changes it everywhere too.

Themes you build yourself show up under **My Themes** in the same section. See [Custom Theme Creator](../Features/CustomThemes.md).

## Artwork colours

Episode cards on the web and desktop apps can take an accent colour from each episode's artwork, for the play button, podcast name and progress. Episode pages and podcast pages use their artwork's colour too, for the play and subscribe buttons, tabs, chapters and progress. It's on by default. Turn it off with the **Artwork colours** switch under **Settings → Change Theme**. Unlike the theme, this choice is saved in each browser rather than on your account. See [Episode Cards](../Features/EpisodeCards.md).
