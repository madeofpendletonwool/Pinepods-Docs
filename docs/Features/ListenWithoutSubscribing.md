# Listening Without Subscribing

You don't have to subscribe to a podcast to use its episodes. Open any podcast, from search, a person's page or a link, and you can play, queue, save, download or transcribe its episodes like any other.

## How it works

- **Web:** play an episode, or open its **⋯** menu, on the podcast's page. The first time takes a moment while your server reads the feed.
- **Mobile:** tap play on an episode, tap it to open its page, or long-press it for its actions.

From then on the episode is a normal Pinepods episode:

- your listening progress is saved and follows you to your other devices
- it can go in your queue, your saved episodes, a collection or your downloads
- it shows up in your listening history and stats
- **Search** in the sidebar finds it once you've played, saved, queued or downloaded it

The podcast itself stays out of your library. It isn't on the **Podcasts** page, its new episodes don't show on **Home** or in smart playlists, it isn't sent to your sync apps (gpodder, AntennaPod, Nextcloud) and your server doesn't keep checking its feed.

Opening the podcast again, for example from an episode in your queue, still shows the **Subscribe** button, with your progress on the episodes you've played.

## Subscribing later

Subscribe the usual way, with the button on the podcast's page. Everything you did before carries over: progress, queue, saved episodes and downloads stay as they were. From then on it's a normal subscription: it appears in your library, refreshes and syncs.

Unsubscribing removes the podcast and what you did with its episodes, as it always has.

## Cleanup

Pinepods forgets a podcast you never subscribed to once nothing of yours uses it any more (no listens, played marks, saves, queue entries, downloads or collection entries for its episodes) and it hasn't been used for 30 days. This runs with the nightly cleanup.

A transcript you made is kept with the episode while the podcast is still around, but it doesn't keep the podcast around by itself. Save the episode or add it to a collection to keep it.

:::note Private and YouTube feeds
This works for regular podcast feeds. Feeds that need a username and password, YouTube channels and local podcasts need a subscription first.
:::
