# Video Podcasts

Some podcasts publish video instead of audio. Pinepods marks an episode as video when its feed gives a `video/…` enclosure; nothing needs setting up.

![PinePods video player](/img/screenshots/videoplayer.png)

- **Web and desktop:** open the full-screen player while a video episode plays and the video shows where the cover art would be. Progress, speed and "played" work as they do for audio.
- **Mobile:** the apps play the episode's sound only.
- **Podcasts page:** video podcasts show a TV icon on their episode count.

Video episodes download like any other episode.

[YouTube channels](./YouTube.md) are different: Pinepods downloads their audio only, so they play as audio everywhere.
